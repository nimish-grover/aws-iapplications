Vision 2047 Focus Areas

  • Integrated Water Management: A holistic approach to river and coastal management, including wastewater treatment, ecological restoration, and community participation, is crucial.
  • Water Governance: Adapting Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM) to state needs and enhancing grassroots community involvement are key.
  • Water Conservation Infrastructure: Developing and maintaining optimal water storage and modernizing existing projects are essential for climate resilience and sustainable supply.
  • Drinking Water for All: Sustaining the Jal Jeevan Mission's focus on drinking water quality and ensuring all households have potable water by 2030, especially vulnerable groups.
  • Irrigation Efficiency: Achieving the "Har Khet Ko Pani" mission through strategic interventions, including micro-irrigation and improved command area development.
  • Smart Water: Promoting water use efficiency across all sectors, adopting water-efficient cropping patterns, and using advanced technology for sustainable agriculture.
  • Digital Water Management: Leveraging data, technology, and innovation, including AI/ML, are seen as key enablers for sustainable water management practices.
